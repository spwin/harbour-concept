Harbour

The paid layer on top of the open Lake protocol. A person's house holds their records (health, money, home) and puts them to work without letting them go: firms bring offers worked out inside the house, researchers pay for answers they only ever see as totals, people team up to buy or refuse together, and their own AI agent keeps watch. Everything the house does (rules, keys, offers, studies, receipts) is the open-source house and control panel. What we sell is on these screens: a hosted house with no setup, data connectors, real payouts, and one console for the firms and researchers who come to people's houses, with a marketplace of modules others build. Click any screen to try it.
